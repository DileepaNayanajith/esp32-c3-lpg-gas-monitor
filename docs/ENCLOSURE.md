# Enclosure design and printing

The enclosure design is the project owner's own work. The owner reports that it was 3D printed at Printpro3D.lk.

The supplied video shows the assembled enclosure with a display window, indicator openings and an attached cable. A frame of this assembly is included in the README. It is a video frame, not an independent photograph or CAD render.

Original CAD and STL files were not supplied. Dimensions, filament material, print settings, fastening method and internal mounting details have not been recovered and are not guessed here. The enclosure has no documented ingress-protection, thermal or product-safety rating in the supplied material.
